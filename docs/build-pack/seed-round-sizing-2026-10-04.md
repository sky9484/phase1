# Raise US$2.0M, park Labuan, cut targets

**Bottom line.** Splash should raise **US$2.0M on a post-money SAFE, equity only (no token warrant)**, asking a **US$10M post-money cap (US$8M pre, 20% dilution)** and accepting **US$8M post at the floor (25%)**. If the market only gives US$7M post or less, shrink the round to US$1.5M instead of selling 28%+. Close **US$1.2M first**, which funds the 20-month Base plan (US$1.22M); leave US$0.8M open as an extension on the same SAFE. The cost side holds up: **Base 20-month spend is US$1.22M with Labuan parked** (ESTIMATE; US$1.42M with a 3-month buffer). Parked Labuan adds **US$0.43M**, and US$2.0M survives that (24.9 months). Sky's "US$3M+" is a US/all-sector headline: at a US$10M post it means 30% dilution, which fewer than 10% of seeds accept, and no SEA or crypto-payments comp supports a US$15M post without live volume. The targets are the weak point, not the budget: 50 LOIs is a stretch, 6 signed payout partners is not credible while the Philippine set is closed, and at month 20 revenue covers only about 4-16% of burn. Series A at month 20 is unlikely. The US$2.0M buys about 31 months, and that runway is what makes a late Series A or a bridge survivable.

### Instrument, tranches, token
- **Post-money SAFE, equity only.** A token warrant adds regulatory liability ([Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2911/2025-02-07-key-considerations-prospective-blockchain-investor)) with no valuation benefit, because Splash has no token. If Colosseum's US$250k SAFE + warrant arrives, take it as a one-off ([Colosseum](https://colosseum.com/accelerator)). Defer any entity flip via a side letter.
- **Drop the milestone-gated tranche.** Tranches are one-way options for the investor and do not syndicate ([SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/is-it-common-to-have-a-vc-fund-seed-round-in-milestones)). The existing gate (3 LOIs + 1 PH partner + US$1M/month) is also incoherent: the LOI test is trivial and the volume test is out of Splash's control.
- **Use close plus extension instead:** US$1.2M first close, then US$0.8M within about 6 months on the same SAFE. If the M6 milestones are hit, the extension cap steps up to US$12M post. Blended dilution is about 21.7% (1.2/8 + 0.8/12).
- **Likely investor mix (ESTIMATE):** one crypto-payments lead at US$0.5-0.75M, an ecosystem cheque, a Cradle-type co-investor (RM1-3M = US$0.25-0.73M, [The Edge](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/782671)), plus angels. Lead scarcity is the binding constraint, not price.

### When an investor pushes back
- **"Too high."** Blox raised US$1M with traction. Answer: US$2.0M is Base spend plus buffer and a regulatory reserve, including the audits that gate mainnet. The ask is at the pre-seed crypto median of US$2.5M, not above it. Offer US$1.5M at US$7.5M post.
- **"Too low, take US$3M+."** More money at the same cap means 30%+ dilution and a harder Series A; the extension absorbs upside.
- **"Where is the token?"** There is none. Equity-only; Colosseum STAMP is the precedent.
- **"Why not fund Labuan capital now?"** Labuan is a trading licence, not a payments one. The perimeter opinion decides, and US$2.0M covers it.
- **"Tranche it."** Close plus extension gives the same discipline without the option.

## 4. Pressure test: the budget is realistic, the targets are not

### 50 LOIs and 6 payout partners
- **Meeting math (REPORTED rates, [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/outbound-sales-conversion-rates), B2B SaaS not payments):** held meeting to deal is 15-21%. 50 deal-equivalents need **240-330 held meetings** (about 320-490 booked), or 14-19 held per month over 17 selling months. If an LOI is 2x easier, 120-165 held (7-10/month). Pure cold email would need 32,000-49,000 sends, which the forwarder universe cannot support. Warm intros, associations and events must carry it (partner and association fees: not found).
- **Cost per LOI (ESTIMATE, GTM note unit prices):**
  - Cash GTM plus SDR: Base US$68k, or **US$1.4k per LOI** (Lean 1.1k, Safe 2.1k).
  - Fully loaded (adds CEO 60%, CCO 25% and CMO payroll): **US$6.4k per LOI** (Lean 4.0k, Safe 7.9k), before contingency. The time-share assumptions are mine.
  - Per **live** customer at 20-30% LOI-to-live (UNSOURCED): US$21-32k at Base fully loaded.
- **Payout partners.** Honest read: **6 LOI-stage conversations are plausible; 6 signed commercial agreements are not.**
  - The Philippine set is closed. BSP's indefinite VASP moratorium plus a 7 Sep 2026 draft 12-month freeze on new operator applications ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/philippines-payment-operator-freeze-vasp-checks), VERIFIED as reported) means partners come from existing holders.
  - Coins.ph already sits inside Circle's CPN ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260201949832/en/Coins.ph-Joins-Circle-Payments-Network-to-Enable-Local-Currency-Payouts-in-the-Philippines)), so the PH leg overlaps Circle itself.
  - Planning bands (ESTIMATE): API-first orchestrators take 2-4 months to sign plus 1-3 to integrate; banks and payment institutions take 6-12 months to pilot. Noah claims 2-3 weeks contract to go-live (REPORTED).
  - Capacity: two engineers, about 80 engineer-months; **2-3 live integrations is the ceiling**, and partner count is not integration count.
- **Revenue at M20 (ESTIMATE):** the user's unit economics (187 businesses, ~US$27M/month) imply ~US$144k per business per month. 10-15 live businesses is US$1.4-2.2M/month volume and **US$2.9-10.7k/month revenue** (plans US$149/499 plus 0.10-0.15% fee) against US$68k burn: **4-16% coverage**. The break-even basis (burn level) is unstated; the defence book's fee-only figure was US$57M/month at US$57k burn.
- **Series A bar:** payments comps are priced on volume (Conduit over US$10B/yr, Bridge over US$5B, Noah over US$1B at seed). The generic non-AI SaaS bar is US$1M ARR floor with 2x growth (weak source). US$1M ARR is about US$83k/month, **above Splash's burn**. Hypothesis (ESTIMATE): US$5M+/month live volume, 15+ paying businesses and 2+ live partners. At month 20, expect a bridge or a volume-story A, not an ARR-story A.
- **Probabilities.** [Carta](https://carta.com/data/linkedin-seed-to-series-a-still-uphill-battle/): best recent cohort 32.6% reach Series A within two years, none at 30%+ since Q3 2021; benchmarks put year two at 15/25/35% (low/mid/high). Plan on **15-25%** for pre-revenue crypto payments (ESTIMATE). My judgement, unsourced: 50 non-binding LOIs about 30-40%, 25 LOIs about 70%, 3-4 signed partners about 50%, 6 signed about 10-15%.

### Top 8 ways this plan fails
1. **No usable payout partner.** The BSP freeze plus the Circle/Coins.ph overlap leaves no PH leg, or one with no pricing leverage.
2. **Perimeter opinion says Splash is a remitter.** MSB capital funds are unknown, e-money needs RM1-5M, Labuan is US$430k, and the budget jumps 35% or more.
3. **LOIs are not volume.** Non-binding LOIs convert at an unsourced 20-30%, and revenue stays near zero.
4. **No lead.** Early-stage crypto funding is at a record low and Malaysia's early pool is thin (about US$41M all-stage H1 2025 ex-Ashita, ESTIMATE).
5. **Runway versus the Series A clock.** 20 months equals the median seed-to-A gap, so the process starts at M12-14 with a weak metric.
6. **Engineering overload.** Three chains, AI agents, audits and 2-3 integrations on two engineers.
7. **Burn creep.** On-costs, above-market hires, FX, audit quotes above the 50k assumption, and placeholder lines that turn out larger.
8. **Squeeze from orchestrators and incumbents.** Circle CPN, Noah, Tazapay, Stripe-Bridge and Mastercard-BVNK all reach the same partners, and a post-audit incident would be fatal.
